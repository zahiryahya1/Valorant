
# Product Vision

## Overview

Valorant Season Recap is a Spotify Wrapped-inspired analytics experience that transforms Valorant match history into a personalized seasonal summary.

The application provides players with meaningful statistics, trends, and highlights that allow them to reflect on their gameplay experience.

---

# Problem Statement

Current Valorant tracking tools provide players with access to raw performance statistics, but many tools focus primarily on competitive analysis and individual metrics.

Players do not currently have a simple, engaging way to view a summary of their season and understand their overall gameplay journey.

---

# Target Audience

The primary audience includes:

## Casual Players

Players who want a fun and engaging summary of their gaming experience.

Examples:

- Total games played
- Favorite agents
- Best performances
- Personal highlights

## Competitive Players

Players who want deeper insights into their performance.

Examples:

- Agent effectiveness
- Map performance
- Rank progression
- Gameplay trends

---

# Product Differentiation

Existing platforms provide detailed statistics and tracking.

Valorant Season Recap focuses on:

- Storytelling
- Personalization
- Season-based reflection
- Fun and engaging insights

The goal is not only to display statistics, but to transform data into an experience.

---

# Success Criteria

Success will be measured in two areas.

## Product Success

A user can:

1. Enter their Riot ID
2. Retrieve their Valorant history
3. Generate a personalized season recap
4. Explore meaningful gameplay insights

## Engineering Success

The project demonstrates:

- A reliable ETL pipeline
- Scalable database design
- Analytics engineering practices
- Professional documentation
- Maintainable software architecture

---

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal is to create a production-quality gaming analytics platform that combines data engineering, analytics, and user experience design.
